O1nk Clips

Stream highlights, clipped automatically.

O1nk Clips is a personal tool that watches live gaming streams (only from creators who permit clipping), detects the moments the audience reacts to most, and turns them into short vertical clips with captions. Clips are posted to the operator's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts after the account owner signs in and grants permission.

It is not a public service: there are no sign-ups, and it only ever posts to accounts its operator has connected.
